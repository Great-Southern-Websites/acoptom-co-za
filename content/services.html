Our Services

At Aretha Coetzee Optometrists we provide comprehensive eye exams, drivers licence screenings, specialised contact lens fittings, glaucoma checks, and family eye care for all ages. We also help you find stylish frames and sunglasses that combine sharp vision with full protection and personal style.

Comprehensive Eye Tests

Get a full, thorough eye exam to determine your exact prescription and detect any early issues. At Aretha Coetzee Optometrists we check vision sharpness, eye teaming, and overall health indicators for clear, confident sight.

Family Eye Care

We provide personalised vision checks for every age, from kids' eye development to adults and seniors. Our approach ensures healthy eyes and timely corrections for the whole family in a welcoming Pretoria setting.

Drivers Licence Screening

Quick and convenient official vision tests for licence applications or renewals. We make the process easy so you can meet requirements without hassle and drive safely.

Specialised Contact Lenses

Expert fittings for all types, including multifocal, toric, and complex cases. At Aretha Coetzee Optometrists we ensure comfortable, clear vision with custom contact lens solutions tailored to your needs.

Glaucoma Screening

Advanced checks to spot early signs of glaucoma and protect your long-term eye health. Our screenings help catch issues before they affect your vision, giving you peace of mind.

Fundus Photography & Eye Health Monitoring

Detailed retinal imaging to monitor eye conditions and overall ocular wellness. We use this tool to track changes over time and support proactive care for sharper, healthier vision.

Prescriptions

Need a prescription? Yes, we handle all your prescription needs with thorough, professional eye exams. At Aretha Coetzee Optometrists we determine your exact prescription for eyeglasses, contact lenses, or both, while checking for eye health issues to ensure clear, comfortable vision.
